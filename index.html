    How the weighted score works

    Each generic material has a level from 1 to 5 for carbon footprint, cost and circularity potential, set from banded thresholds over the whole database. Lower is better for carbon and cost; higher is better for circularity. Each level is turned into a sub-score from 0 to 1:

    carbon_sub      = (5 − carbon_level) / 4
cost_sub        = (5 − cost_level) / 4
circularity_sub = (circularity_level − 1) / 4

score = 100 × Σ (weight × sub_score) / Σ weight   (only criteria that have data)

    If a material has no data for a criterion, that criterion is left out of its score and the cell shows "–". Materials with no data for any weighted criterion are listed last. With all weights at 0, results stay in search-relevance order.
