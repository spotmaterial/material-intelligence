About

A database of materials for design: families and grades with physical, mechanical, thermal and environmental properties, relative ratings for carbon footprint, cost and circularity potential, and the rules, certifications and labels to check when using them. It also lists material innovations from companies.

This site is a research tool built in the RED-Pro project. It offers search with filters, user-weighted ranking, material pages with rules and certifications, and an API for reading the data.

Data

  • Property values were compiled by Rob Thompson from handbooks, datasheets, papers and environmental declarations.
  • Values are indicative ranges for generic materials, not specifications for a particular product.
  • Rules and certifications describe what to check for each material; they do not say that a product complies.
  • Records are reviewed on a rolling basis and corrected as errors are found.
